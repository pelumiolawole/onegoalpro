fix: duplicate-retry guidance, evolution date mislabel, honest landing page stat

- task_generator.py: give the "duplicate" retry path concrete alternative
  material (forbidden verb, approved verb list, explicit recent titles)
  instead of vague "be different" guidance. Confirmed live in production
  July 12: a duplicate retry reproduced the same title verbatim under the
  new lower retry temperature, which improves compliance when paired with
  concrete material but can backfire into repetition when guidance is vague.

- evolution/page.tsx: fix "TODAY dot old-date" mislabel. The word "Today"
  was hardcoded next to the user's most recently COMPLETED task's date, not
  the actual current date, so any user who hadn't completed a task recently
  saw a false "today" label on an old date. Not a caching or date-math bug;
  the date was always correct, only the label was wrong. Now shows "Today"
  only when the date genuinely is today, "Most recent" otherwise.

- page.tsx (landing): replace unverified "100+ people have committed to one
  goal" with "99 people have started their transformation. And counting."
  Verified via Supabase: 99 total registered users, 49 with an active goal,
  3 past the actual commitment gate. "Committed" overclaimed against all
  three readings. 99 is the accurate count for "started."
